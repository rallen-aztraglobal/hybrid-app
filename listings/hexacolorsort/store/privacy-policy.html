Privacy Policy — Hexa Color Sort

Effective date: 21 August 2026

Hexa Color Sort (“the app”) is a puzzle game. This policy explains what the app collects and who receives it.

Stored only on your device

Your best score and your sound and vibration preferences are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never transmitted and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Collected and shared with third parties

Advertising identifier and installation data. The app includes the AppsFlyer and Adjust measurement SDKs. They collect your device’s Google Advertising ID, device model, operating system version, app version, install referrer, and app open and session events, and send them to AppsFlyer and Adjust so that we can attribute installations to marketing sources. The app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission for this purpose.

Notification token. The app includes Firebase Cloud Messaging. On launch it obtains a Firebase registration token for this installation and sends it, with your device’s operating system version, to our server so that notifications can be delivered to this device.

Configuration request. On launch the app requests its configuration from our server. The request contains the platform name, the application identifier, and your device’s time zone name (for example Asia/Manila). As with any internet request, our server also receives the originating IP address.

Not collected

The app does not request or collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera input, precise or device location, or any account credentials. There is no sign-up and no login. The app contains no advertising placements and no in-app purchases.

Third parties that receive data

  • AppsFlyer — installation attribution and marketing measurement
  • Adjust — installation attribution and marketing measurement
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — notification delivery

Each processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Notifications: decline the permission prompt, or turn notifications off later in Android Settings › Apps › Hexa Color Sort. The game is fully playable either way.
  • Advertising ID: reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of data associated with your device, contact us at the address below and include the approximate date of installation.
