## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
Hexa Color Sort is a calm, one-handed color sorting puzzle built for short sessions.

How it works:
- Tap a stack to pick up the run of matching colors on top.
- Tap another stack to move them across.
- Get five of the same color stacked in a row to clear them.
- Chain clears quickly to build combos and score more.

What you get:
- Simple one-thumb controls, portrait only
- Every board is generated with at least one legal move
- Undo any move, restart any time
- Combo scoring that rewards planning ahead
- Local best score, no account needed
- Dark, low-glare visuals for late-night play

No timers pushing you around, no lives to wait for. Think a move ahead, keep the
stacks tidy, and see how long a combo chain you can hold together.
