# Privacy Policy for Color Stack

Effective date: 2026-05-10

Color Stack respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect
Color Stack does not collect, store, or share personal data.

## Analytics and Tracking
Color Stack does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Account and Login
Color Stack does not require account creation or login.

## Children's Privacy
Color Stack does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Sharing
No personal data is collected, therefore no personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Security
Because no personal user data is collected by the app, there is no personal data stored on remote servers by Color Stack.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes in future versions, updates will be reflected in this document with a revised effective date.

## Contact
For privacy-related questions, contact: support@colorstack.app
