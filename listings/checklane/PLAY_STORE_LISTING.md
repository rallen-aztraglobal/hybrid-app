```

## 简短说明（≤80）

Checklists you run again and again. Tick, reset, run it. Not a to-do list.
```

（73 字符）

## 完整说明（≤4000）

```
A to-do list is for things you do once. A checklist is for the things you do every
time — and those are the ones you forget.

CheckLane is built for the second kind. Tick your way down the list, then clear the
ticks and run the same list again tomorrow. The items stay; only the ticks go.

WHAT IT IS FOR

• Before you leave — keys, wallet, charger, windows
• Packing for a trip you take often
• Opening and closing a shop
• Handover at the end of a shift
• Pre-flight, pre-drive, pre-stream — anything with a "did I..." attached to it

RESET IS THE POINT

Most list apps make you delete each item, or copy the list, or tick and untick one by
one. Here it is one button: the ticks clear, the list stays exactly as you wrote it.
And it can be undone, because clearing a list you were halfway through is exactly the
kind of mistake that needs taking back.

MADE FOR REAL USE

• As many lists as you like, each with its own progress
• Duplicate a list to use it as a template — the copy comes back unticked
• Drag to reorder items and lists
• The whole row is the tick target, not a small box — you are usually walking
• Ticked items dim and get a line through them, so what is left stands out
• Thirty steps of undo, covering deletes as well as ticks

NO ADS. NO SIGN-UP. WORKS OFFLINE.

There are no adverts, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Nothing is uploaded, so it
works with no connection at all.

PRIVACY

CheckLane does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and requests no camera,
microphone, location or contacts permission. Your lists — the titles and items you
type — stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
```

## 写文案时守住的三条

1. **不写做不到的事。** 没有提醒/通知、没有截止日期、没有云同步、没有共享协作、
   没有子任务、没有附件，就一句都不提。这几样是待办类 App 的标配，
   用户会默认有 —— 更要在文案里避免暗示。
2. **"Not a to-do list" 这句是卖点也是护栏。** 它先把预期摆正：
   来找提醒功能的人不会下载，也就不会因为没有提醒而给差评。
3. **隐私那段要写明「你打的字」。** 本包存的是用户自由输入的清单标题与条目，
   不是几个整数。含糊成「不存任何个人数据」就与实现不符了。
