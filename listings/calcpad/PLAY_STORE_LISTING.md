## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
CalcPad is a straightforward calculator for the arithmetic you actually do every day.

What it does:
- Add, subtract, multiply and divide
- Percentages
- Sign toggle for negative numbers
- Decimal point
- Backspace to fix a single mistake, AC to start over

Why you might like it:
- Big round keys sized for one-handed use
- The equation you are building stays visible above the result, so you can see
  what you typed before you commit to it
- Dark, low-glare interface that is easy on the eyes at night
- Opens instantly and does nothing else in the background
- No account, no sign-in, no onboarding
- Portrait layout, works offline

That is the whole app. No scientific mode you never use, no history you have to
manage, no banners covering the keypad. Type the numbers, read the answer, close it.
