# Privacy Policy for CalcPad

Effective date: 2026-08-28

CalcPad ("the app") is a calculator. This policy explains what the app collects
and who receives it.

## Stored only on your device
Nothing. The app keeps no history, no saved results and no settings. What you
type exists only while the app is open.

## Collected and shared with third parties

**Advertising identifier and installation data.** The app includes the AppsFlyer
and Adjust measurement SDKs. They collect your device's Google Advertising ID,
device model, operating system version, app version, install referrer, and app
open and session events, and send them to AppsFlyer and Adjust so that we can
attribute installations to marketing sources. The app declares the
`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission for this purpose.

**Notification token.** The app includes Firebase Cloud Messaging. On launch it
obtains a Firebase registration token for this installation and sends it, with
your device's operating system version, to our server so that notifications can
be delivered to this device.

**Configuration request.** On launch the app requests its configuration from our
server. The request contains the platform name, the application identifier, and
your device's time zone name (for example `Asia/Manila`). As with any internet
request, our server also receives the originating IP address.

## Not collected
The app does not request or collect your name, email address, phone number,
postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone or camera input,
precise or device location, or any account credentials. There is no sign-up and
no login. The app contains no advertising placements and no in-app purchases.
The numbers and calculations you enter are never transmitted anywhere.

## Third parties that receive data
- AppsFlyer — installation attribution and marketing measurement
- Adjust — installation attribution and marketing measurement
- Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — notification delivery

Each processes the data it receives under its own privacy policy.

## Your choices
- Notifications: decline the permission prompt, or turn notifications off later
  in Android Settings › Apps › CalcPad. The calculator works either way.
- Advertising ID: reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in
  Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
- Uninstalling the app removes it entirely; there is nothing stored to delete.

## Children
The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from
children.
